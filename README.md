# Muto Agent Skills
Skills and workflows by [Muto](https://muto.ai) that help AI understand you and work with you.

## Your personal brand
Build a living collection of your stories, goals, experience and preferences, then use it with AI to create work that sounds like you. Start with a warm welcome and a progressive interview. Develop useful assets early and improve them through use.

The Personal Brand skill includes a living knowledge-base template, interviewing guide, source-based voice guidance, 24 asset template recipes, a presence roadmap, progress tracking, Fuel experiences and a local visual workspace.

**Current version: 0.6.10 · Early pilot release**

## Download and start
1. [Download the Personal Brand skill ZIP](downloads/personal-brand-system-v0.6.10.zip?raw=true). Extract it on your computer.
2. Open `personal-brand-system/README.md` for getting-started instructions.
3. Open `personal-brand-system/assets/dashboard/PB-personal-brand-dashboard.html` in your browser to explore a clean example. No server is needed for viewing.
4. Give your AI the skill folder if it supports skills, or share `SKILL.md` and `references/kickoff-guide.md` in the conversation. Other referenced files can be supplied as needed.

Start with this message:

> Help me develop my personal brand using this skill. Start with a warm welcome, explain how I can use it for everyday work, and offer the visual workspace with opening instructions. Ask one question at a time. Work in a separate personal copy. If you cannot save files, give me updates I can save and tell me which supporting files you need.

## Your personal strategic plan
Turn reflection into an annual focus, six core Target Objectives, and a focused quarter across God, Fit, Fam, Biz, $, and Up. The guided process works one pillar at a time and can reuse accepted Purpose, Tenets, and personal style from your Personal Brand System.

**Current version: 0.3.1 · Compact dashboard update**

1. [Download the Strategic Plan skill ZIP](downloads/personal-strategic-plan-v0.3.1.zip?raw=true) and extract it.
2. Open `personal-strategic-plan/README.md` for getting-started instructions.
3. Open `personal-strategic-plan/assets/dashboard/strategic-plan-standard.html` in your browser to explore the blank offline view.
4. Give your AI the skill folder using its supported method and ask it to create a separate private personal copy for your chosen year.

Start with:

> Help me create my personal strategic plan using this skill. Begin with my Current Year Wrap & Recap, one pillar at a time. Draft from my answers, ask one focused question at a time, and use a separate private copy. Reuse my accepted Personal Brand System foundation and style if available. Offer the dashboard with opening instructions, and distinguish saved work from proposed updates.

Includes calendar-aware quarterly pacing, personal focus-word definitions, independent tracking/weight decisions, quarter locking and activation, optional source images and reflection templates, native Obsidian templates, and a read-only customization-aware upgrade preview. Targets and templates are personalized in the private copy. Version 0.3.1 adds a compact Overview, distinct pillar cards, clearer annual targets, grouped quarterly support, and bulleted Tenets with bold opening phrases.

## Your voice notes

Turn recorded WAVs into structured Obsidian notes with local Voicebox transcription, a retained audio archive and optional action tracking. Use it as a starting point for your own recorder, vault and workflow.

**Current version: 0.1.0 · Early pilot · Apache-2.0**

1. [Download Voice Note Intake Portable](downloads/voice-note-intake-portable-v0.1.0.zip?raw=true) and extract it.
2. Read the [getting-started guide](skills/voice-note-intake-portable/README.md) and [architecture overview](skills/voice-note-intake-portable/references/architecture.md). The initial setup targets Windows with Python 3.11+, PowerShell 7, Obsidian and local Voicebox.
3. Give your AI assistant the extracted skill folder and start with:

> Use Voice Note Intake Portable to inspect my setup, ask for missing settings and test with a sample recording in an isolated vault. Keep recorder deletion and scheduling disabled.


## Your files and your control
This repository contains only the reusable standard and a clean example. Keep your personal knowledge, answers and generated assets in a separate private folder outside this repository. You choose what to share with your AI platform and review anything before it becomes public.

The files and workspace can live locally. External AI processing follows your chosen platform's settings; the skill does not make all AI execution local. This package is freely available. AI platform costs may apply. Native skill loading, file saving and supported uploads vary by platform. File-capable environments can initialize your personal copy and rebuild the workspace; the included helpers require Python for these operations. Plain chat can guide you and produce proposed updates for manual saving.

## Pilot feedback
Tell us what helps, what feels too deep and what does not sound like you. [Open an issue](https://github.com/muto-labs/agent-skills/issues) with feedback about the standard. Do not post private stories, customer information or personal knowledge-base files in public issues.

Package checks and interface checks have passed locally. Beginner usability, timing and compatibility across AI platforms continue to be evaluated. The skill is a living approach that improves through feedback.

## Updating your workspace
Your AI saves the source files and rebuilds the workspace. You then reload the open HTML page. Reloading alone cannot rebuild it. Use Update my view next to See what's next for a request to copy to your AI, opening instructions and a Reload saved view button. A temporary preview is not a public website or a substitute for a saved handoff.

## Existing users: update safely
Download the new standard separately. Do not extract it over your personal folder. Follow [the update guide](skills/personal-brand-system/references/update-guide.md): preview, backup, preserve personal files and merge customized conflicts before rebuilding and reloading.

Version 0.6.10 separates purpose and personal tenets into independently reviewed assets. The overview uses canonical approved purpose wording and shows personal tenets only after explicit approval. Draft tenets stay hidden. Existing personal knowledge, review decisions and legacy assets are preserved through the standard safe-update workflow.
