## Action ledger
ID | objective | channel/action | owner | priority | effort | dependency | target timing | state | artifact/version | approval | live URL | verification evidence/date | next step.
States: planned, in progress, blocked, deferred, complete. Artifact states are separate: draft, reviewed, approved for specified public use, published, verified, stale, archived.
## Phases
Foundation: KB and positioning. Expression: voice and starter assets. Presence: prioritized profile/website work. Cultivation: contribution, relationships and reviews. Select scope based on capacity; no requirement to claim every account.
## Approval record
Leader | artifact/version | channel/use | date | scope | subsequent material change.
Mark complete only with evidence appropriate to the action: approved draft for a drafting action; live verification for a publishing action.
