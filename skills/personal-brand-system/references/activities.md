# Fuel — advanced brand amplifiers
Choose the experience that helps your current goal. These apply across leadership contexts, but not every leader needs every experience. No completion target or expansion of baseline onboarding. Revisit as your goals and experience evolve.

- [Origin & history studio](fuel/origin.md): Turn pivotal experiences into stories that explain who you are.
- [Reputation mirror](fuel/reputation.md): Compare the impression you intend with the signals people see.
- [Voice lab](fuel/voice.md): Experiment with language until the words feel unmistakably yours.
- [Trust lab](fuel/trust.md): Make credibility, reliability, connection and care visible in your actions.
- [Opportunity compass](fuel/opportunity.md): Choose opportunities that fit your goals, values and capacity.
- [Contribution portfolio](fuel/contribution.md): Build a credible collection of the impact you have helped create.
- [Purpose exploration](fuel/purpose.md): Explore where your interests, strengths and contribution meet.
- [Audience journey](fuel/journey.md): Make it easier for people to understand how you can help.
- [Future lab](fuel/future.md): Explore a possible future and turn it into a practical experiment.
- [Relationship experience design](fuel/relationships.md): Design thoughtful interactions that help people feel understood.
- [Audience field test](fuel/field-test.md): Test a message or asset with people you want to serve.

## Everyday practices
Reflection recorder: capture an event and lesson in 5–10 minutes; separate fact from interpretation and propose KB updates.
Asset freshness map: after KB changes, review affected assets and a refresh plan in 15–30 minutes; check dependencies.
Agent briefing: give AI the task, audience and relevant KB in 10–20 minutes; check disclosure and missing context.
These remain available through maintenance and everyday application rather than Fuel cards.

## Fuel status
Persist each amplifier's status in fuel-state.json: ready (Ready to explore), active (In progress), usable (Ready to use), revisit (Revisit). Set active when the leader starts an experience; usable when its output is reviewed for use; revisit when a relevant change or review identifies a refresh need. Do not infer completion from related foundation answers. New packages start ready. Update after actual work and rebuild the dashboard. Status is independent of onboarding coverage; Needs focus shows active and revisit.
