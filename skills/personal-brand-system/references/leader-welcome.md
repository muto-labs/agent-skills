# Welcome to your personal brand
You already have a story. This workspace helps you understand it, put it to work and keep improving it.

## Start with a conversation
Return to the AI chat where you opened this skill. Tell it what you want your brand to help you accomplish. Rough answers are welcome. You can skip a question, ask for examples or say “we have enough—let’s make something useful.”

The dashboard shows saved work. Answer questions and request changes in your AI chat; clicking a card opens information rather than starting an interview or saving an edit.

## Find your way around
- **Overview:** your profile, purpose, channel shortcuts and foundation coverage.
- **Knowledge:** your saved brand knowledge and any voice or visual guides available so far.
- **Fuel:** deeper exercises to use when they help your goals. You do not need to do them all.
- **Assets:** profiles, bios, posts, graphics and other work, with review status.
- **Progress:** open foundation items and work parked for later.
- **Skill components:** the tools and reference material behind the experience, if you want to explore them.

Each section has its own view inside this one saved file. Your browser’s Back button can return to the previous view. Close details with × or Escape.

## Keep your view current
Ask your AI to save agreed changes and rebuild the dashboard, then reload it. Use **Update my view** for a request you can copy into your chat. Refreshing alone does not save or rebuild anything.

If your AI works in a cloud workspace, ask for the newly rebuilt HTML and complete personal package as downloads. Download and extract the package, then open the dashboard HTML in your browser. Keep the package for future edits. A temporary preview is different from a saved file or a published website.

## Keep growing
You can create useful work before all foundation items are complete. Review what sounds like you, correct what does not and keep accepted examples for next time. Completion measures the initial foundation; your stories and assets continue to grow through use.

Your files belong to you. Choose what to share with your AI and review each asset before public use. Local files do not mean every AI runs locally. If your tool cannot save files or rebuild the dashboard, ask it to explain the smallest manual step or what file-capable help is needed.
