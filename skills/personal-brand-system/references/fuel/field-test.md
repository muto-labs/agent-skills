# Audience field test
Test a message or asset with people you want to serve.

## When it helps
You want evidence that your positioning or content actually connects.

## Estimated effort
20–30 min plus feedback. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
A draft, intended audience and a learning question. What should a reader understand or do?

## Create and review
A small feedback plan, attributed findings and a proposed revision. AI simulation is rehearsal, not audience evidence; do not contact people without authorization.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
