# Origin & history studio
Turn pivotal experiences into stories that explain who you are.

## When it helps
You want a richer origin story or material for talks.

## Estimated effort
45–90 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Timeline, turning points, influences, choices and lessons. What changed your direction?

## Create and review
A timeline and three story cards. Check chronology, individual contribution, consent and disclosure.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
