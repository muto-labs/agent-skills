# Purpose exploration
Explore where your interests, strengths and contribution meet.

## When it helps
You are reconsidering your direction or what matters next.

## Estimated effort
30–45 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Interests, strengths, needs and economic realities. What would be worth exploring?

## Create and review
Purpose hypotheses and small experiments. Income is not required for meaning; no diagnostic or universal personality claims.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
