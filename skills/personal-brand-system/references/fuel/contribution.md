# Contribution portfolio
Build a credible collection of the impact you have helped create.

## When it helps
You need stronger proof for a profile, talk or opportunity.

## Estimated effort
30–45 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Work and service examples. Who benefited, how, and what was your contribution?

## Create and review
Evidence cards with contribution and limitations. Verify claims and distinguish team outcomes from individual credit.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
