# Reputation mirror
Compare the impression you intend with the signals people see.

## When it helps
Your public presence may not reflect your current direction.

## Estimated effort
20–40 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Profiles, work and actual feedback when available. What impression do these support?

## Create and review
A gap map and one improvement. Separate AI interpretation from real audience evidence.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
