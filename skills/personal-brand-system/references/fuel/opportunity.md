# Opportunity compass
Choose opportunities that fit your goals, values and capacity.

## When it helps
You are weighing a role, partnership, invitation or commitment.

## Estimated effort
15–25 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Invitation, objectives, values, capacity and current roles. What would this cost and contribute?

## Create and review
A fit rationale and decision options. The leader owns the decision; acknowledge unknowns.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
