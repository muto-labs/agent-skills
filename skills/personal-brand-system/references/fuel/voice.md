# Voice lab
Experiment with language until the words feel unmistakably yours.

## When it helps
Your drafts feel generic or your voice is evolving.

## Estimated effort
20–30 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Writing samples or preference choices. Which treatment sounds like you, and why?

## Create and review
Accepted examples and practical voice rules. The leader must recognize the result; samples are not mandatory.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
