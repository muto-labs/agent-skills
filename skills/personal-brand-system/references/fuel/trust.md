# Trust lab
Make credibility, reliability, connection and care visible in your actions.

## When it helps
You want to strengthen the experience of working with you.

## Estimated effort
20–40 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Examples of credibility, reliability, connection and safety, care and service. What commitment matters?

## Create and review
Evidence, practices and commitments for each dimension. No calculated trust score or unsupported claims.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
