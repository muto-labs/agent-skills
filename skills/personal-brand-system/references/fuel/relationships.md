# Relationship experience design
Design thoughtful interactions that help people feel understood.

## When it helps
You want meetings, follow-ups or gatherings to offer more value.

## Estimated effort
20–40 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Audience needs, personal style, trust practices and capacity. How should someone feel and what should they leave with?

## Create and review
An experience outline and reusable preparation/follow-up asset. Keep it genuine, feasible and consent-aware; outreach requires authorization.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
