# Future lab
Explore a possible future and turn it into a practical experiment.

## When it helps
You want to connect a longer-term ambition to action today.

## Estimated effort
20–40 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
A 1–5 year possibility, assumptions, constraints and current strengths. What could change, and what could you test now?

## Create and review
A scenario, explicit assumptions and one small experiment. Label speculation; define a useful learning signal.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
