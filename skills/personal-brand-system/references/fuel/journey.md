# Audience journey
Make it easier for people to understand how you can help.

## When it helps
People struggle to see where to start or what comes next.

## Estimated effort
30–45 min. Planning aid, not a deadline.

## Explore
Audience goals, problems and your contribution. What is their next useful step?

## Create and review
A guide, plan, invitation and outcome narrative. Avoid manufactured fear and guaranteed outcomes.

Save the draft, proposed KB changes, leader decision and one next action. Reuse relevant existing knowledge; ask only for material gaps. This is an advanced amplifier chosen for a current goal, not an onboarding requirement. Repeat when useful. Nothing becomes public without review.
