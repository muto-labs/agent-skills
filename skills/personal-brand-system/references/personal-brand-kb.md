## Record conventions
Knowledge records: ID | statement | type (confirmed fact / interpretation / aspiration / unknown) | source/date | leader review | disclosure (private / review required / public approved).

Asset records: ID/path | template/version | KB version/record IDs | status | review | publication context/URL | verification date.
Do not treat "public approved" knowledge as blanket approval for a new public artifact.
