# Voice, style and vibe guide
ID: voice-guide | Version: 0.2.0
Audience/use: AI writing and editorial review.
Required inputs: Samples or preferences; accepted edits; contexts.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: 600–1000 words.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Voice traits; tone by context; diction/rhythm; structure; humor; avoid; before/after; confidence.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: Prefer concrete verbs. Example: 'Choose an owner' rather than 'Drive ownership alignment.'

## Validate and adapt
Rules demonstrated by examples; distinguish observed from aspirational; no samples required.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.


## Source-based calibration
Follow [voice preservation](../../references/voice-preservation.md). Include a small phrase bank, source-backed rules with confidence, and raw-to-edited examples with leader feedback. Use interview language when writing samples are absent. Do not replace distinctive phrasing with generic corporate language or use detector scores.
