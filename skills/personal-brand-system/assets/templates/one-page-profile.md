# One-page profile
ID: one-page-profile | Version: 0.1.0
Audience/use: Potential collaborators and leader review.
Required inputs: Identity; positioning; evidence; messages; next step.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: 400–650 words.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Introduction; purpose; who I help; approach; proof; principles; contact/next step.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: Purpose: help teams turn unclear ownership into workable commitments.

## Validate and adapt
Skimmable, coherent, evidence-linked; contact only if supplied.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.
