# Visual direction
ID: visual-direction | Version: 0.2.0
Audience/use: Design work.
Required inputs: Intended use and available preferences; missing materials do not block a provisional direction.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: Brief plus reference inventory.

## Generate
Follow [the two-track visual start](../../references/visual-start.md). Reuse existing materials or offer a minimal beginner comparison. No logo or full brand pack required.
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Working visual direction: personal/company/combination context; desired feeling; accent and neutral colors; one font/fallback; likes/dislikes; restrictions; proposed/accepted-for-use/deferred status; next real use. Add imagery or broader design detail only when requested or needed.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: Direction: clear, approachable, uncluttered; test readable contrast.

## Validate and adapt
No need for logo; references are inspirations, not copied assets.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.
