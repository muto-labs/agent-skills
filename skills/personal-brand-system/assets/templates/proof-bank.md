# Proof and claim bank
ID: proof-bank | Version: 0.1.0
Audience/use: Internal brand knowledge.
Required inputs: Claims; evidence; source dates; attribution; permission.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: Table plus short evidence cards.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Claim → evidence → leader contribution → limitations → disclosure → permitted use.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: Claim: clarified ownership. Evidence: supplied project retrospective; impact pending verification.

## Validate and adapt
Never turn team results into individual credit; uncertainty explicit.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.


Follow [canonical claims and wording controls](../../references/integrity-guide.md). Preserve evidence status, attribution, measurement units and uncertainty. Record claim IDs and fixed-language versions for each asset; mechanical validation does not replace factual review.
