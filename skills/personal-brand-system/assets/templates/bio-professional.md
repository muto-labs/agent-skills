# Professional bio
ID: bio-professional | Version: 0.1.0
Audience/use: Customers, partners and professional introductions.
Required inputs: Positioning; current roles; supported proof; voice.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: 80–150 words.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Contribution → relevant role/context → one proof point → invitation.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: Maya helps operations teams simplify handoffs. Her work combines workflow design with practical team coaching.

## Validate and adapt
Accurate roles and attribution; no unsupported superlatives; swap proof for each audience.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.
