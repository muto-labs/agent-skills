# Narrative bio
ID: bio-narrative | Version: 0.1.0
Audience/use: Website and longer introductions.
Required inputs: History; positioning; selected evidence; disclosure.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: 250–400 words.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Current contribution → meaningful turning point → approach → evidence → present direction.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: A confusing project handoff led Maya to study how teams share responsibility.

## Validate and adapt
No forced adversity; story serves reader; private events excluded.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.
