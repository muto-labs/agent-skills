# Story introduction
ID: pitch-story | Version: 0.1.0
Audience/use: Speaking and networking.
Required inputs: Turning point; contribution; audience relevance.
Optional inputs: audience-specific preferences and verified examples.
Length guidance: 30–60 spoken seconds.

## Generate
Read relevant KB records and voice guide. Ask only for material gaps; if unavailable, mark them visibly in the draft. Choose disclosure-appropriate proof. Follow this structure flexibly:
Brief moment → lesson → current contribution → conversation invitation.

## Illustrative example
Fictional; not a leader fact: After watching a handoff stall a project, I started asking what makes ownership clear.

## Validate and adapt
One moment; no inflated claim; audience-relevant invitation.
Adapt audience, tone and length without changing factual claims. Record source IDs, KB/template versions and review status in the asset registry. No public use until the leader reviews and approves that version/context.
